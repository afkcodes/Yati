### **Palette 1: Vibrant Sunrise**
This palette uses bright, uplifting colors inspired by a sunrise, while maintaining readability and accessibility.

1. **#FFFFFF** (White)  
   - **Usage:** Background color for a clean and fresh base.
2. **#FF6F61** (Coral)  
   - **Usage:** Primary buttons or headers; vibrant and attention-grabbing.
3. **#FFD166** (Golden Yellow)  
   - **Usage:** Secondary buttons or highlights; adds warmth and energy.
4. **#6B5B95** (Soft Purple)  
   - **Usage:** Accent color for icons or progress bars; adds depth and sophistication.
5. **#2E5266** (Dark Teal)  
   - **Usage:** Text or borders; provides strong contrast and readability.

**Accessibility Check:**  
- Coral (#FF6F61) on White (#FFFFFF) has a contrast ratio of **4.5:1** (meets WCAG AA standards).  
- Dark Teal (#2E5266) on White (#FFFFFF) has a contrast ratio of **7:1** (exceeds WCAG AA standards).

### **How to Apply These Palettes:**
1. **Contrast:** Use darker shades (e.g., Dark Teal, Dark Blue) for text on light backgrounds and lighter shades for text on dark backgrounds.
2. **Hierarchy:** Use vibrant colors (e.g., Coral, Turquoise) for primary actions like buttons and muted tones (e.g., Slate Blue, Soft Purple) for secondary elements.
3. **Consistency:** Stick to the chosen palette across all screens to create a cohesive and harmonious design.
4. **Accessibility:** Always check contrast ratios using tools like [WebAIM Contrast Checker](https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/) to ensure readability for all users.
